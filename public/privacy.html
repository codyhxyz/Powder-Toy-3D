Privacy and terms

Updated October 2026

Powder Toy 3D runs in your browser, and you can play without an account. Your scenes, settings and saved constructions are kept in this browser's storage, not on our server.

Signing in

Signing in with Google gives you more free AI generations a day. When you sign in, we store:

  • which service you signed in with, and your account id there
  • your email address, name and profile picture address
  • how many AI generations you used each day

Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in. We don't use cookies. You need to be 13 or older to sign in.

The free AI

When you generate with Powder Toy AI, your prompt and pictures of the construction go through our server to OpenAI, which writes the code. Our server doesn't keep your prompts. OpenAI stores each request and its answer for up to 30 days, so a construction can be built over several steps, and handles them under its own policies. We also send OpenAI an anonymous ID, so it can spot abuse without knowing who you are.

To count free generations for players who aren't signed in, we keep a salted hash of your IP address for one day. We never store the address itself.

Your own keys

If you bring a key for another AI provider, it stays in your browser and goes only to that provider. It's never sent to us, put in links or shared with other players.

Multiplayer

Shared worlds pass through our relay while you play. We don't save them.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. That removes your account and its sign-in details from our server right away. Signing out only ends the session in this browser.

Terms

Powder Toy 3D is free and comes as is, without guarantees. The free AI has daily limits and may change or stop. Don't use it to make anything illegal or to get around its limits. We may block accounts that do.
