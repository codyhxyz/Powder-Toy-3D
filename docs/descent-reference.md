# Descent: what Noita's first biomes are made of

Notes from studying the Noita new-game map (the user's copy; crops live only in a
session scratchpad, never in the repo). This is the content brief for the Descent
Wang kits (`src/world/wang/kits/`). The single biggest lesson: **Noita's biomes are a
dark open void with floating masses of material in it, not solid rock with tunnels
carved through it.** Every formation below sits in air, with air under it.

## Mines (Kaivos)
- Structure: one huge dark cavern (background rock only) filled with stacked,
  floating horizontal **earth shelves** (2–6 cells thick, ragged edges), grass and moss
  on their tops, roots and drips hanging under them.
- Shelves are held up by **timber**: vertical posts down to the shelf below, beams
  along their edges, cross-braced scaffolds, diagonal timber stairs between shelves,
  ladders.
- **Mine carts** on beam rails along shelf edges.
- **Water tanks**: wooden basins on shelves full of water; small pools in shelf dips;
  a flooded bottom.
- **Explosive crates** (skull boxes): small wood/metal boxes of gunpowder/TNT on
  shelves.
- **Lanterns** hanging under shelves (here: wall torches).
- **Collapsing sand/earth masses**: huge loose sand bodies at the biome's edges that
  slump when disturbed.
- Hearts, chests, gold in the earth, pink gem clusters lower down, toxic moss patches.

## Coal Pits (Hiilikaivos)
- Structure: dark purple void with **floating ribbons and blobs of grey rock/coal**,
  winding and branching, thick enough to walk on and inside.
- **Gold flecks everywhere** in the rock (NUGGETS / GOLD specks).
- **Water and toxic pools sitting inside the rock blobs** (basins in the rock).
- **Loose red/pink powder heaps**, often fenced in small **timber cages**.
- Timber planks bridging gaps, **scaffold towers** in the void, ladders.
- A **swing / hanging platform** on ropes over a drop with an item on it.
- Mine carts, coal piles, red fungus tufts on top surfaces, glowing orbs.
- Next to it (Noita's Fungal Caverns): glowing cyan mushrooms, green slime/toxic
  channels winding through earth.

## Snowy Depths (Lumen alla)
- Structure: dark void with **floating marbled ice/snow islands**, every island's
  underside fringed with **long icicles**.
- **Dark water pockets inside the islands** (frozen-over or liquid).
- **Wooden cabins hanging on chains**, tilted, with gold and skeletons inside
  (treasure rooms you break into).
- Snow drifts and mounds on tops, a snowman, plank bridges, a shop stand with wands.

## Set pieces the user asked for
- **Acid pits with treasure inside**: a basin of (green, thick-looking) ACID with gold,
  a chest or a wand at the bottom — you have to get it out without dissolving.
- Rare magic-liquid pools (Teleportatium, Levitatium, Berserkium, Healthium, Whiskey).
