# Factory construction

Select **Factory → Open-Cut Quarry** in the map menu.
The starter line separates gold from sand and ships the gold for credits.

## Controls

- **Build:** open construction beside the camera controls. This interface is Factory-only.
- **Machine tile:** select a piece and leave dismantling mode.
- **Click:** place the selected machine at the preview.
- **Rotate:** turn the preview by 90°.
- **Dismantle:** switch to removal mode. Click a machine to remove it for a full refund.
- **Done:** close construction. Escape also closes it.
- **Right-drag:** move the construction camera.
- **God / 1st / 3rd:** choose a camera from the toolbar.

Machines do not use E. There are no B or Shift+B construction shortcuts.
The illustrated catalog scrolls on small screens.
The preview requires clear space; its arrows show the material flow.
Refractory blocks stack into platforms, catch walls, and chutes.

## Production

Gold nuggets pay one credit each. Lifetime earnings unlock equipment:

| Earnings | Unlock |
| --- | --- |
| 12 | Mining agent and grain lift |
| 48 | Pump |
| 96 | Furnace |

A mining agent breaks the nearby face and moves debris toward its rear.
The drill reaches four cells beyond its deck. Reposition it when that face is empty.
Conveyors move loose grains. The separator sends its selected material forward and other grains to the right.

The furnace melts scrap and pours it forward. A pump can move water or molten metal.
A separate catch keeps the furnace from reheating finished metal.
Water provides cooling, but the reservoir is finite.
Metal at 400°C or below pays four credits per grain.
Ship 32 metal grains after unlocking the furnace to complete the quarry. Freeplay continues afterward.

## Sandbox and saves

The build interface does not appear in Sandbox.
Existing Sandbox machines and saved pump cargo still work.
Spark-powered machines use the existing batteries, conductors, and switches.
Factory machines use their built-in drive.

Saves include machine placements, progression, and liquid held inside pumps.
If a transfer is pending, pause and wait before saving.
Factory saves require an empty held-tool load; pour or throw that material back into the quarry first.
Sandbox terrain undo does not undo individual machine placements. Clear/Undo restores the complete machine state.

This version supports bounded box maps, not streamed World maps.
There is no offline production. Sandbox access marks a retained Factory run as Sandbox-assisted.
