Raster bench

Powder Toy 3D v2, week 0: what v2's frame costs on this device, drawn with 4× MSAA and with TAAU at ⅔ resolution.

Takes about a minute per scene. Keep the screen on and the page in front.

What it measures

  • The scene's real cells (the game's lab or volcano preset at 128³), drawn as v2 draws them: a sun shadow map, the opaque faces, then liquids and glass at 1× over the resolved colour and depth, then the tone map.
  • MSAA: the opaque pass at the output size with 4× MSAA, resolved, its samples discarded. TAAU: the opaque pass at ⅔ size, jittered, then a resolve that reprojects history and upscales.
  • Each is run with 1 and 2 frames in flight, and MSAA also with transient attachments where the browser has them. The camera follows the same orbit in every run.
  • GPU time per pass comes from timestamp queries where the browser has them; otherwise from the time between submitting a frame and the GPU finishing it.
  • The verdict: if the MSAA frame costs more than 1.5× the TAAU frame, v2's default anti-aliasing becomes TAAU (docs/v2.md, section 11).
